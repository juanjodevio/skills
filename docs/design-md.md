# design-md attribution

Generic semantic Design guidance in this suite follows Google’s **`design-md`**
pattern: atmosphere, principles, visual system, interaction, content,
responsive behavior, accessibility, assumptions, and unknowns as durable Design
direction.

## What this suite does

- `steer-design` owns the configured Design Steering artifact.
- Templates and procedure cover the semantic sections above when relevant.
- Empty ceremonial sections are omitted.
- Visual implementation, screenshots, and assets are evidence only until
  maintainer intent settles them.

## What this suite does not require

- **Stitch is not required.** Do not install, invoke, or depend on Stitch.
- Do not copy Stitch-specific retrieval or generation behavior.
- Design adapters must remain usable without any Stitch tooling.
